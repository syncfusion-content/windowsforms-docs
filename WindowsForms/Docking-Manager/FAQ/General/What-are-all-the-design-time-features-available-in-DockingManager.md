# What are all the design time features available in WinForms Docking Control

## Dragging and docking at DesignTime
WinForms Docking Control supports dragging and docking of the dockable controls at the design time itself. It also lets you float the controls.

 ![Dragging and Docking](General_images/General_img3.jpeg)

## Property settings using task window
The Task Window of the WinForms Docking Control at design time lets you apply certain Appearance and behavior settings.

 ![Property settings](General_images/General_img2.jpeg)
